Getting a single answer from a large language model is a common task. However, many business processes are much bigger than a single question.

Automating a complex business process, like researching a new sales lead or managing thousands of customer support tickets every day, requires more than a simple back-and-forth conversation. It requires a system that can think, plan, and act on its own.

This is where designing agent architectures comes in. An agent architecture is a blueprint for an intelligent system that can tackle multi-step problems autonomously. It is like moving from giving instructions to a single assistant to designing and managing an entire team of specialized experts.

Why This Matters
Designing an agent architecture allows for the solution of real-world problems that are too complex for a single prompt.

A sales research agent could browse a company's website, find key employees, and scan recent news—all at the same time—delivering a complete report in minutes instead of hours.
An intelligent customer support system could instantly categorize incoming tickets, route them to the right department, and find solutions in a knowledge base, ensuring no customer has to wait long for help.
These systems do not just answer questions; they complete tasks and achieve goals.

What You Will Learn
This module covers the architectural thinking behind intelligent systems. It explores how to decompose a complex problem and design a system of cooperating agents to solve it. The outcome is the ability to analyze a business challenge and outline a plan for an autonomous system to handle it, setting the foundation for building powerful, agent-driven applications.
